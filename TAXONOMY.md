# Topic taxonomy for James Clear 3-2-1 entries

Assign 1 to 3 topics per entry from THIS LIST ONLY, spelled exactly. Pick the topic(s) the entry is really about, not words it happens to contain. First topic = the primary one.

1. Habits & Consistency — building/keeping habits, systems vs goals, environment design, small daily actions, identity-based habits, routines, showing up.
2. Action & Getting Started — taking action, starting now, imperfect starts, speed, momentum, doing vs planning, procrastination.
3. Focus & Priorities — focus, saying no, eliminating, simplicity, minimalism, choosing what matters, to-do lists, distraction, commitments.
4. Time & Mortality — shortness of life, seizing the day, aging, youth, how to spend your days, time as an asset, living one day at a time.
5. Learning & Curiosity — reading, books, teaching, beginner's mind, asking questions, learning faster, knowledge vs wisdom, curiosity, education.
6. Mastery & Craft — practice, quality, doing great work, creativity, art, writing, inspiration, genius, craftsmanship, professionals vs amateurs.
7. Career & Business — work, jobs, leadership, strategy, entrepreneurship, building a business, sales, teams, culture, high performance.
8. Money & Wealth — money, spending, saving, investing, real wealth, luxury, status, leverage.
9. Relationships & Friendship — friends, community, who you surround yourself with, belonging, trust, neighbours, mentors, peers.
10. Love & Family — romantic love, marriage, parenting, children, family, grief for loved ones.
11. Kindness & Generosity — kindness, generosity, helping others, charity, gratitude toward others, raising others up, forgiveness.
12. Mindset & Attitude — optimism, hope, positivity, lightness, gratitude, focusing on what you can control, personal responsibility, excuses, blame, attitude toward circumstances.
13. Emotions & Inner Life — anger, anxiety, worry, stress, rumination, self-pity, jealousy, grief, peace of mind, calming the mind, mental spirals.
14. Self-Knowledge & Authenticity — knowing yourself, being yourself, values, self-esteem, self-acceptance, self-love, boundaries, your own path, what you really want.
15. Failure & Resilience — failure, mistakes, setbacks, comebacks, mental toughness, perseverance, persistence, hardship, bad days, bad luck, endurance.
16. Criticism & Doubt — handling critics, feedback, self-doubt, comparison to others, feeling unqualified, looking foolish, reputation.
17. Risk & Decisions — decision making, risk, uncertainty, luck and chance, margin of safety, bets, reversible vs irreversible, judgment.
18. Happiness & Meaning — happiness, contentment, purpose, a good life, joy, savoring, wanting less, meaning of life, fulfilment, peace.
19. Health & Energy — exercise, walking, the body, rest, sleep, energy, longevity, nature, food.
20. Thinking Clearly — perspective, wisdom, judgment, questioning assumptions, open mind, mental models, paradoxes, seeing clearly, bias, first principles.
21. Change & Growth — personal change, reinventing yourself, discomfort of growth, seasons of life, incentives for change, becoming who you are, growth vs comfort.
22. Success & Ambition — defining success, greatness, winning, hard work, potential, excellence, ambition, competition, being the best, long-term thinking.
23. Communication & Influence — conversation, listening, giving advice, persuasion, negotiation, asking for what you want, changing minds, motivating others.

Rules:
- Every entry gets at least one topic. Use the closest fit even if imperfect.
- Prefer 1–2 topics. Use 3 only when the entry genuinely spans three.
- Questions: tag by what the question makes you reflect on.
- Quotes: tag by the quote's message (the "lead" line often names the subject).
